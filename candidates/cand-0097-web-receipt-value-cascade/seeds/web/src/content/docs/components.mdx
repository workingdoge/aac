The design system ships as **web components** — custom elements themed by the
verified paintgun tokens. Three carry the institution.

## The record

The core object is a signed record — a journal voucher — not a screen. Every
record states who signed, what moved, which ledger changed, and what proof
exists. Debits equal credits; the class is zero; `pacioli_equal` holds.

## The proof terminal

Identical in light and dark — **proofs are invariant**. The proof surface
records what the verifiers discharged, never what a proposer claims.

## The receipt

The clearing object is a **BalancedVectorReceipt**:
[EVENT-COMPLETE/1](/specs/applications/event-complete-1/) compiles a typed
commercial event into a canonical journal, [VNET/1](/specs/applications/vnet-1/)
proves selected amount vectors net by basis dimension, and
[SPARSE-CELLS/1](/specs/profiles/sparse-cells-1/) fixes the sparse witness
shape. USD balances with USD, fabric with fabric, garment with garment; nothing
collapses through price. Role coverage is proven, and the receipt carries its
own proof.
