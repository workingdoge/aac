Tag 120 (receipt-reference leaf) is realized by `circuits/rulebook`'s
`event_commitment` — the committed commercial-object/event instance. Tags 124 and
125 (cand-0032) reassign the participant-set and event-nullifier structures off
the 122/123 collision with the receipt-reference block (3/PROOF §3: tag reuse
across structures is a soundness failure); they are distinct structures, so they
take their own tags rather than overload 122/123.

Tag 126 (cand-0039) is the event-commitment leaf of the SECOND posting program,
`circuits/bom-receipt` (a bill-of-materials / materials-kit receipt over basis
B=4). It takes its own tag rather than reuse 120 because `event_commitment` does
not bind the `posting_program_id`, so a shared tag could let two schemas share a
commitment preimage — a per-schema tag keeps them distinct (3/PROOF §3). (A future
governance option is to bind `program_id` into `event_commitment` and collapse to a
single leaf tag; deferred — it would change the committed values.)

Tags 127/128 (cand-0040) are the OPEN and CLOSE legs of a FICC/GSD repurchase
agreement (`circuits/repo`), the first real clearing instrument on the EVENT/1
harness. A repo is two balanced exchanges (basis B=2: cash + security par), time-
separated; each leg gets its own event leaf because each is a separately cleared
Event (the cross-leg sequencing — close consumes the open position — is a
TRANSITION/1 concern, and the multilateral net of many such legs is VNET/1's).
